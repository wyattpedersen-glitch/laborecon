# Study sites

Two single-page study sites, one per course. Open the `index.html` in a browser. Math renders via MathJax from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed. Checkboxes, drill streaks and the current tab persist in the browser.

## `index.html` · Econ 155A (Cities and Public Policy), Midterm 1 — the live site

Covers lectures 1–9, the stated exam scope: why cities exist, agglomeration, city size, urban growth, place-based policy, bid rent and land use, housing and housing policy.

Tabs:

- **Learn** – the default view: seven guided lessons plus one optional for a from-zero learner, in the order that scores, each with the idea in plain words, the one graph, the attached papers, and say-it-back questions; ends with the 11 findings to memorize and the 14 to reason out.
- **Plan** – an evening-and-morning schedule that follows the lessons, with persistent checkboxes.
- **Practice exam** – the 2026 practice midterm (8 T/F, 3 short answers) with model answers and redrawn graphs.
- **Drills** – 16 generators with random numbers, answer checking, worked steps and streaks, split into Core (graph-story choices and directly askable numbers) and Optional (HW-style algebra).
- **Lectures** – for each lecture group, a short "what is realistically testable" list, with the full notes folded underneath as reference only.
- **Papers** – the 30 review-sheet papers in Question / Approach / Findings format, plus a one-line table of everything else cited in lecture.
- **Problem sets** – HW1 and HW2 restated with full solutions.
- **Flashcards** – 48 cards rated Missed / Got it; ratings persist and reorder the deck so misses come back first.
- **Cheat sheet** – the testable core only.

## `econ151/index.html` · Econ 151 (Labor Economics), Midterm 1

Covers lectures 2–5: competitive equilibrium, technological change, education, compensating differentials. Plan, Recipes, Drills, lecture tabs, research designs, 11 papers, problem sets, practice exam, flashcards, cheat sheet.

## `econ151/midterm2.html` · Econ 151 (Labor Economics), Midterm 2

Covers lectures 6–8: employer market power, wage-setting (posting vs bargaining), and regulation (minimum wages, noncompetes, pay transparency). Plan, Attack plans with mnemonics, 15 randomized Drills, lecture tabs, 12 papers with go-to picks by research design, research designs, HW3 worked solutions, the official practice question plus a full practice exam, flashcards, cheat sheet.
